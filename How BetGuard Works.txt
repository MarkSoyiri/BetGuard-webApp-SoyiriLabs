HOW BETGUARD WORKS
=========================================================================

WHAT IT IS
BetGuard is a free companion app that helps you bet responsibly. It is
NOT a betting platform — you cannot place bets, deposit money, or win
real money through it. It is simply a tool to help you understand and
control your own betting habits.

WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU

1. Track every bet
   You log each bet you place: which platform, which sport, how much
   you staked, and whether it won or lost. Over time you build a clear
   picture of your real spending.

2. Keep you within budget
   You set a monthly betting budget (in GH₵). BetGuard shows a live
   gauge of how much of that budget you have used, and warns you as you
   get close to your limit.

3. Turn betting money into savings
   Instead of staking money you would have bet, you can add it to a
   savings goal — like an emergency fund or a new phone. BetGuard shows
   your progress toward each goal.

4. Teach you good habits
   - Daily, weekly, and monthly challenges (e.g. "No Bet Today") that
     you complete and claim rewards for.
   - Educational articles and a short quiz on responsible betting and
     money basics.
   - A risk assessment quiz that tells you whether your habits are
     Low, Medium, or High risk, and gives personal advice.

5. Give you a personal AI coach
   You can chat with the built-in coach. It reads YOUR numbers (your
   spending, win rate, budget status) and answers questions like "How
   much have I spent this month?" or "What should I do after a big
   loss?".

6. Keep you connected and motivated
   - A community feed where you can share stories and tips.
   - Achievements (badges) you unlock as you build better habits.
   - Notifications that remind you of budgets, challenges, and wins.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
Everything is stored locally on YOUR device (your browser's
localStorage). There is no account database or server, so nothing is
uploaded anywhere. If you clear your browser data, BetGuard starts
fresh. You can also export or reset your data from the Settings page.

HOW TO GET STARTED
1. Open the app (the Vercel link) and click "Get Started".
2. Sign up (any details work — it's a demo) and you'll land on your
   Dashboard.
3. Log a bet from the "Betting Log" page to see your stats change.
4. Set a monthly budget, create a savings goal, and take the risk
   assessment.
5. Explore the AI Coach, Challenges, Education, and Community pages.

DEMO LOGIN
Because there is no real backend, the demo accepts any email and
password, and gives you a sample profile ("Alex Mensah") pre-loaded with
70 days of demo betting history so the dashboard looks alive from day
one.
